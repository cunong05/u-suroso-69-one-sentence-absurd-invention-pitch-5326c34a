One-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance, entered in the [One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a) bounty on make.fun.

## The entry

> The Ransom Charger locks your phone at 1% until you admit out loud that you should have charged it last night.

110 characters, one sentence. The annoyance: always letting your phone die at the worst moment. The invention: a charger that takes your phone hostage at 1% and only releases it once you confess — out loud — that you should have charged it last night. The laugh comes from the charger itself: it doesn't fix your battery, it extorts an apology out of you first.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22+, standard library only, no dependencies) writes the entry deterministically to `output/entry.txt`. Running it twice produces byte-identical output (no trailing newline).
